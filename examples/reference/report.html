Method and interpretation

Mean error bars show one sample standard error. Δ vs TWAP uses paired seed differences; a negative number means lower cost. Tail estimates from 128 seeds are noisy: the worst 5% spans only 6.4 observations, with fractional boundary weighting.

Search uses 32 candidates and 32 training seeds per candidate. Evaluation starts at seed 1000007; search and evaluation seeds are disjoint for stress runs. Neighbors change only market-order intensity by ±10% within the search bounds.

External event times, prices, quantities and cancellation IDs are frozen before execution. Policy orders affect replayed queues and fills, but do not change external arrival rates. Fees are explicit per-filled-share inputs, not calibrated exchange schedules. No empirical calibration, price prediction, live trading or GPU performance is claimed.
